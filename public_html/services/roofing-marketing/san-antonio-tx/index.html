The local angle

Paid ads in a storm market

In a calm month, a San Antonio campaign is mostly repair, replacement and financing searches. After a hail event, storm and claim searches take over, click prices jump and out-of-town competitors flood the auction for a couple of weeks.

A campaign that's set up once and left alone wastes money both ways: too little budget when demand spikes, too much on broad searches when it's quiet. Answer rate matters too — with LSA moving into Google Ads, some unanswered calls can still be billed.
