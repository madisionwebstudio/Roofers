The local angle

Why San Antonio SEO is its own problem

The Maps pack is driven by distance from your business address. In a metro this wide, a roofer on the North Side and one near Lackland can both rank #1 — for completely different homeowners. One rank number for “San Antonio” tells you almost nothing.

After a hail event, searches like “hail damage roof” and “roof inspection near me” spike for days. The roofers who already rank for those terms in the hit zip codes take most of that demand before ads even ramp up.
