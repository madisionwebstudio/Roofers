The problem

Your crew is on a roof. The phone is in the truck.

Roofers miss calls for good reasons: you're on a roof, driving, in a homeowner's attic, or it's 7 PM. The homeowner doesn't know that. They see a ringing phone and move to the next name.

The second leak is the estimate. You drive out, measure, write a five-figure quote — and then nobody follows up because everyone's busy. The roofer who checks in on day two and day five tends to get the signature.
