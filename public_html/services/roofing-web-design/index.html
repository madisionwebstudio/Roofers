The problem

Most roofing sites are built to look nice, not to get calls.

Homeowners find you on a phone, often standing in a wet living room. If your site takes five seconds to load, hides the phone number or asks for 12 fields before they can request a quote, they hit back and call the next roofer.

A lot of roofing websites are also rented: the agency owns the domain or the hosting, and leaving means starting from zero. We don't build that way.
