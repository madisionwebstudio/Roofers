What a San Antonio roofing site has to do

After a big storm, homeowners get door hangers from crews they've never heard of. Most of them will look you up before they call. The site has a few seconds to show recent local jobs, real reviews and a number to tap.

Many of those homeowners are dealing with an insurance claim for the first time. A clear, Texas-specific claim explainer builds more trust than any “Google Guaranteed” badge — and it keeps people on your site instead of a competitor's.
