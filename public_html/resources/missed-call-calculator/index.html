How the math works

Missed calls per month = calls per week × 4.33 × % missed. Of those, only some are real roofing leads — the rest are suppliers, sales calls and wrong numbers, which is what the “real leads” slider is for.

Jobs lost = real leads missed × your close rate. Revenue lost = jobs lost × average job value. It doesn't count referrals those homeowners would have sent, or the ad spend you paid to make the phone ring in the first place.

How to find your missed-call rate

Open your phone system or call-tracking dashboard and count last week's inbound calls that weren't answered live. If you don't have a dashboard, check the recent-calls list on the business phone. If you can't find a number at all, that's a leak too.
