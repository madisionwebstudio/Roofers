Why roofers

Why we narrowed down to roofing

We spent years building websites and running ads for plumbers, HVAC companies, landscapers and other local businesses. Roofing kept standing out, for two reasons.

First, the leads are expensive. Roofing has the highest cost per lead of any home-service trade on Google, so every missed call hurts more than it does for a plumber.

Second, the follow-up is broken almost everywhere. Crews are on roofs. Office managers juggle permits, insurance adjusters and supply orders. Calls go unanswered and estimates go cold — not because anyone is lazy, but because nobody built a system for it.

That's a problem we know how to fix. So we stopped working with everyone else.

Principles

Six rules we don't bend

  1. Signed jobs over rankings

    If it doesn't show up as a call, an inspection or a signature, it isn't the headline in your report.

  2. You own everything

    Domain, website, Google Business Profile, ad accounts, data. In your name from day one.

  3. Prices on the page

    You shouldn't need a sales call to find out what something costs.

  4. Month to month

    After 3 months, we keep your business by earning it every month.

  5. No fake proof

    No invented reviews, logos, testimonials or results — on our site or yours.

  6. Say it plainly

    Short updates, real numbers, and “that didn't work” when it didn't.
