The lead leak

Your marketing isn't the problem. The leak is.

Most roofers we talk to don't need more leads first. They need to stop losing the ones they already paid for. Here's where they go.

  1. 01

    Missed call

    You're on a roof, in the truck, or it's 7 PM. The call rings out. Most homeowners don't leave a voicemail — they call the next roofer on Google.

  2. 02

    No callback

    Someone sees the missed call hours later. By then the homeowner has two inspections booked, and you aren't one of them.

  3. 03

    No follow-up

    You hand over a five-figure estimate and wait. No text on day 2, no call on day 5. The roofer who followed up gets the signature.

  4. 04

    No tracking

    Nobody can say which ad, keyword or page produced the jobs that signed — so budget goes wherever the agency says it's working.
