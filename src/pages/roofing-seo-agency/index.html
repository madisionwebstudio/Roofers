Local SEO for roofers

What a roofing SEO company does in month one.

Most roofing SEO problems are the same handful of fixable things. The first month is about fixing them before writing anything new.

1. Audit what Google already sees

We check which searches you already show up for in Google Search Console, where you rank in Google Maps across your service area, and how your Google Business Profile compares with the three roofers already in the Maps top 3 for your main town.

2. Fix the Google Business Profile

Primary category set to roofing contractor, every service listed with a description, service areas that match the towns you actually work in, accurate hours and real job photos. No keywords stuffed into your business name — that's a common reason profiles get suspended.

3. Fix the website basics

Each service gets its own page — roof replacement, roof repair, storm and hail damage — with a title and heading that match what homeowners search. City references always include the state. Schema markup tells Google you're a roofing business and where you work. Slow pages, broken links and indexing problems get fixed.

4. Make your name, address and phone consistent

We write down your official business details once and make them identical on your website, Google, Bing, Apple Maps, Facebook and the key directories. Small differences confuse search engines.

5. Start the review routine

Reviews feed Maps rankings and decide whether homeowners tap your listing. We set up a simple routine to ask every happy customer — with a direct link — and we reply to every review.

From month two, the work shifts to new service and city pages, local links and steady improvements to the pages that are close to page one. Every month you get a plain report showing calls, form leads and rankings — so you can see exactly what your roofing SEO services are doing.

Do I need SEO if I have a website?

Yes, if you want Google leads. A website shows homeowners you're legit; SEO is how they find you in the first place.

How long does roofing SEO take?

Expect early movement in 1–3 months and strong results in 3–6 months. Competitive metros can take longer. Need calls this week? Pair SEO with Google Ads.

Do you guarantee #1 on Google?

No — nobody honest can. Google ranks businesses on relevance, distance and prominence, and changes how it ranks all the time. We guarantee the work and show you every result in plain reports.

How do you get roofers into the Google Maps top 3?

A complete, accurate Google Business Profile, a steady flow of genuine reviews, consistent citations, and service and city pages on your website that match what homeowners search. Distance from the searcher still matters — we can't change that, so we focus on everything else.

Will you write fake reviews or buy links?

Never. Fake reviews break Google's rules and FTC rules, and bought links can get a site penalized. We help you ask real customers for reviews and earn links from real local sources.

What do I keep if I stop?

Everything: your Google Business Profile, every page we wrote, your citations and your data. SEO plans are month to month.
