The Atlanta roofing market

Storms, trees and fast-growing suburbs.

Atlanta is known for its tree canopy, and that shapes roofing here. Thunderstorms and high winds bring down limbs and whole trees onto roofs, while humidity and shade encourage algae streaks and moss. Spring and summer storms also bring hail to parts of the metro.

The metro keeps growing outward — Cobb, Gwinnett, Forsyth, Cherokee and Henry counties — and every suburb has its own searches and Maps results. Many of those suburbs are full of homes built in recent decades, with roofs now reaching replacement age.

Atlanta homeowners also see a lot of roofing marketing. A site that looks established, shows real local projects and answers questions plainly is how a smaller company competes.
