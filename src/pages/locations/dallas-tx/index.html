The Dallas roofing market

Hail capital energy, storm-chaser competition.

Dallas–Fort Worth is one of the most hail-exposed metro areas in the country, and spring storm season shapes the whole year for local roofers. When a hailstorm crosses Collin, Dallas or Denton County, thousands of homeowners search for inspections at once — and storm-restoration companies from out of state arrive within days.

It's also one of the biggest roofing markets in the US: NextTerritory estimates the Dallas market at roughly $2.03 billion a year. Big markets attract big competitors, so the Maps top 3 and the top of Google Ads are fought over by well-funded companies with thousands of reviews.

For an independent Dallas roofer, that means winning on proof and speed: a website that shows you're local and established, a Google profile that keeps earning reviews between storms, and ads you can switch on the morning after hail.
