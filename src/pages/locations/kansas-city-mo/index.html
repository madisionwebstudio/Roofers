The Kansas City roofing market

Hail alley, split by a state line.

Kansas City sits in one of the most hail-prone parts of the country, and spring storms regularly bring hail and high winds across the metro. Storm seasons can bring large waves of roofing demand.

The metro is split between Missouri and Kansas — Overland Park, Olathe and Lenexa on the Kansas side; Lee's Summit, Independence and Blue Springs on the Missouri side. Homeowners search by their own city, and the rules differ by state.

Kansas requires roofing contractors to register with the state Attorney General's office under the Kansas Roofing Registration Act. If you work on the Kansas side, showing your registration number is a simple trust signal.
