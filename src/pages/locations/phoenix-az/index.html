The Phoenix roofing market

Heat, sun and monsoons — and a lot of tile.

Phoenix roofing is different from most of the country. Many homes have concrete or clay tile roofs, and flat roofs with foam or coatings are common. Tile itself can last a long time, but the underlayment beneath it wears out from years of extreme heat and UV — so “tile underlayment replacement” and roof coatings are big parts of the market.

Monsoon season (officially June 15 to September 30) brings dust storms, sudden wind and heavy downpours, and with them a jump in searches for leak repair and storm damage. The rest of the year, demand is steadier and driven by aging roofs across the Valley.

Arizona roofing contractors are licensed by the Registrar of Contractors (ROC). Showing your ROC license number clearly is one of the simplest ways to earn trust on a Phoenix roofing website.
