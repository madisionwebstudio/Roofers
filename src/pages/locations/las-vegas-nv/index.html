The Las Vegas roofing market

Desert heat, tile roofs and HOA rules.

Las Vegas roofs face some of the harshest sun and heat in the country. Tile and flat roofs are common, and — as in Phoenix — the underlayment beneath tile often wears out long before the tile itself. Summer monsoon storms bring sudden wind and rain that expose weak spots.

The market stands out in Groundwork Demographics' roofing market rankings, partly because of high roofing spending per listed competitor. In other words, there's real demand relative to the number of roofing companies competing for it.

Much of the valley is master-planned communities with HOAs — Summerlin, parts of Henderson and many newer developments. HOA rules on materials and colors are a common question, and a roofer who explains the approval process clearly earns trust. Nevada roofing contractors are licensed by the State Contractors Board.
