The Chicago roofing market

Snow, ice, flat roofs and a huge market.

Chicago is one of the largest roofing markets in the US — NextTerritory estimates it at about $2.67 billion a year. Winters bring heavy snow, ice dams and freeze-thaw cycles that open up weak spots, and spring and summer storms bring wind and hail to the suburbs.

The housing stock is unusual too: brick bungalows, two-flats and three-flats with flat or low-slope roofs sit alongside suburban homes with shingles. Many roofers here do both flat-roof and pitched-roof work, and homeowners search for them differently.

Illinois requires roofing contractors to be licensed by the state (IDFPR), and the City of Chicago has its own roofing licensing and permit requirements. Showing your license details plainly is a quick trust win.
