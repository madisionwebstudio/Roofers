Why own your leads

Shared leads are a race to the bottom.

Many roofing lead companies sell the same homeowner's request to more than one contractor. You end up racing others to the phone and competing on price from the first call — and when you stop paying, the leads stop and nothing stays with your business.

Roofing lead generation done right works the other way round. The homeowner finds your company, reads your reviews and contacts you. Every month you keep the website, the rankings, the ad account history and the reviews — so the system gets stronger instead of starting from zero.
