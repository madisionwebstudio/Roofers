SEO FAQ

Honest answers.

Do you guarantee first-page rankings?

No, and you should be wary of anyone who does. Google decides rankings using hundreds of signals — including your reviews, your distance from the searcher and what your competitors do — and it changes them constantly. We guarantee the work we list, and we report on results honestly every month.

How long until I get calls from SEO?

Most roofers see momentum in 3–6 months. In very competitive metros it can take longer. If you need calls this month, pair SEO with Google Ads or Local Services Ads while it builds.

Do I need a website from you to buy SEO?

No, but your site has to be fast, mobile-friendly and editable. If it isn't, we'll tell you — and the free preview is an easy way to see what a new one would look like.

Will you write fake reviews or buy links?

Never. Fake reviews break Google's rules and the FTC's, and bought links can get a site penalized. We help you ask real customers for reviews, and we earn links from real local sources.

What do I own if I stop?

Everything: your Google Business Profile, every page and post we wrote, your citations and your Analytics data. Nothing is held hostage.
