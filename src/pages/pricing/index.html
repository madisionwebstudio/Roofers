Pricing FAQ

Money questions.

When do I actually pay?

After you've approved the finished website — not after the preview, and not before. The site launches on your domain once the first payment ($199 for the monthly plan, or $1,499 one-time) is made.

What happens if I want to leave the $199/month plan early?

The monthly plan has a 12-month minimum, because it replaces a large upfront fee. If you need to end it early, you pay the remaining months of the 12 and the site is yours. After 12 months you can stay month to month or take the site and go.

What does the $29/month after year one cover on the one-time plan?

Hosting, security, backups and updates — the same care that's included in the first 12 months. It's optional: you can move the site to any host you like instead.

Are there setup fees?

Not on websites or SEO. Google Ads has a one-time $300 setup for the account, tracking and first campaigns.

Are SEO and Ads contracts?

They're month to month. The only reason to sign 6 months of SEO is the bonus: 3 extra landing pages free.

Do you mark up ad spend?

No. The ads run from a Google Ads account in your name, billed to your card by Google. You pay us only the management fee.
