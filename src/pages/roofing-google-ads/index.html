Roofing PPC management

What your roofing Google Ads campaigns look like.

Good roofing lead generation through Google Ads comes from structure. We build three core campaigns, each with its own keywords, ads and landing page — so your budget goes to the jobs you want most.

Roof replacement

Your highest-value jobs. Searches like “roof replacement,” “new roof cost” and “roofing contractor” plus your towns. Ads lead with what homeowners worry about on a big project — licensing, warranties, financing — and land on your roof replacement page.

Roof repair

Urgent, smaller jobs that often turn into replacements. Searches like “roof leak repair” and “roof repair near me.” Ads push the phone call, and the schedule matches when someone can answer.

Storm & hail damage

Built and ready before the storm. Searches like “hail damage roof inspection” and “storm damage roof repair.” When a storm hits your area, we narrow targeting to the neighborhoods that were hit and raise the budget the same day.

What we block

Negative keywords stop your ads from showing for searches that will never become jobs: “roofing jobs,” “roofer salary,” “DIY roof repair,” “roofing supplies,” “shingle prices,” “roof for car.” We review the search terms report every week and keep adding to the list — it's where most wasted money is saved.

Who Google Ads is right for

Google Ads works best for roofers who can take on more jobs, have a website that turns clicks into calls, and can commit at least $2,000/month in ad spend for a few months so the campaigns can learn. If your website isn't ready, we'll tell you — and show you a free preview of one that is.

Setting up and running your Google Ads: keyword and town targeting, negative keywords, ad copy, landing pages, conversion tracking for calls, messages and forms, and weekly reports. $399 setup + 10% of ad spend.

Are roofing ads worth it?

For most roofers, search ads on Google are the fastest way to get leads from homeowners who need a roofer now. They work best with a strong landing page and tight targeting. Read roofing SEO vs Google Ads to decide where to start.

The minimum ad budget is $2,000/month, paid directly to Google. Roofing clicks are expensive in most US markets, and a campaign needs enough clicks each month to learn what works. Below that, results are mostly luck.

Usually within the first 1–2 weeks after launch. The first month is also when we learn which searches and towns pay off, so cost per lead usually improves after it.
