Ads FAQ

Before you spend a dollar.

See all pricing.

Why do you recommend $1,500+ a month in ad spend?

Roofing clicks are expensive in most US markets, and a campaign needs enough clicks each month to learn what works. Below that, results are mostly luck. We'll look at your market on the first call and tell you if it needs more — or less.

What's the difference between Google Ads and Local Services Ads?

Google Ads are text ads in search results; you pay per click. Local Services Ads appear above them with a Google badge, and you pay per lead instead of per click. LSA requires Google's verification. Many roofers run both.

Do you guarantee a number of leads?

No. Leads depend on your market, your budget, the season, the weather and your reviews — none of which we control. We guarantee the work and clear reporting on what your money bought.

Who owns the ad account?

You do. It's created in your name and billed to your card by Google. If you leave, you keep the account, the history and every campaign.

Can you run ads to my current website?

Yes, if it's fast and makes calling easy. If it doesn't, ads will waste money — which is why we'll suggest a free website preview first.
