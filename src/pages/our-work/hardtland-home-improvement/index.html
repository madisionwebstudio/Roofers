The client

A local roofer, not a storm chaser.

Hardtland Home Improvement was founded by Tom Degenhardt in 1972 and still runs as a family business in Madison and Verona, Wisconsin. They do roof replacement and repair, storm and hail damage, metal roofing, siding, gutters, and windows and doors — and they help homeowners with insurance claims.

They have the things homeowners look for: decades of local work, a strong Google rating, manufacturer certifications and an owner on every job. Their old website just didn't show them.

What we found

The old site, measured.

  • One page. All 8 menu links (About, Gallery, Services…) jumped around the same page — so there was no page for Google to rank for “roof replacement Madison” or any town.
  • No headline (H1) on the homepage, and a 689-character meta description — Google shows roughly the first 155.
  • Slow on phones: mobile speed score 51, main content after 6.5 seconds, 81 files loaded for one page.
  • Trust buried: no Google rating, certifications or “since 1972” proof on the first screen.
  • Accessibility score 53: 5 of 12 images had no description, and the page blocked pinch-zoom on phones.

What we changed

Built around what homeowners check first.

Every change maps to a question a homeowner asks before calling a roofer.

01

Trust on the first screen

Headline “Madison & Verona's roofer since 1972,” their Google rating, CertainTeed Master Shingler and Owens Corning certifications, and “licensed, bonded & insured” — all visible before scrolling.

02

A page for every job and town

8 service pages (replacement, repair, storm & hail, metal, free inspection, siding, gutters, windows & doors) and 6 town pages (Madison, Verona, Fitchburg, Middleton, Oregon, Mount Horeb), each with its own keyword title and headline.

03

One clear next step

A “Book a free inspection” button in the header and hero, a sticky bar on phones, tap-to-call links, and a short 5-field inspection form on its own page.

04

Local SEO built in

Keyword-first titles like “Roof Replacement Madison WI,” descriptions under 160 characters, RoofingContractor and FAQ schema, city + state on every page, an insurance-claims page and a roofing FAQ.

05

Fast and accessible

Homepage down from 81 files to 9, compressed images, descriptions on every image and pinch-zoom allowed again — mobile speed and accessibility both at 100.

06

Real photos, real reviews

A projects gallery and a reviews page showing what neighbors say — and an owner-run About page telling the 1972 story.
