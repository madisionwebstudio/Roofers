Website FAQ

Before you book.

More on the pricing page.

What do you need from me for the preview?

Fifteen minutes on the phone, your logo if you have one, and a few photos of your best jobs if you have them. If you don't have photos yet, we'll use high-quality roofing photography and mark which ones to swap later.

What counts as a “round of changes”?

One round is one list of changes — as long as you like — that we make together. Swap photos, rewrite a section, move things around, change colors. You get two rounds on the full site. Small edits after launch are included on the monthly plan.

Is it WordPress?

No. Your site is hand-built, fast and secure, with a simple admin panel made for roofing companies. There are no plugins to update and no theme that breaks when something else updates.

Will I lose my Google rankings if you replace my current site?

We do everything we can to protect them: we keep your domain, carry over pages that already get traffic, and redirect every old page address to its new one. Rankings can move a little during any rebuild; we watch Search Console closely after launch.

Do you write the text?

Yes. We write every page for roofing, based on our call and your services and towns. You review it all and can change anything — through the rounds of changes, or yourself in the admin panel.

Can I use my own domain and email?

Yes. The site launches on your domain. If you don't have one, we'll help you register it in your own name. Your email stays where it is.
